# Engines

An engine declares its niche, supported content types, default tone and factual-review policy.
The built-in engines are `football`, `geo`, `music`, `clips`, `politics`, and `trends`.
They share a typed contract for visual scene choices, editorial guidance, and review checks.
The generated plans are local drafts. Their facts and media rights require human verification.
New engines register with the engine registry; core routes do not contain niche branches.
